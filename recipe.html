Delicious Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 caup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix flour, sugar, baking powder and salt
  2. In another bowl,beat egg with milk and melted butter
  3. Combine wet and dry ingredients and mix until smooth
  4. Heat a skiller and pout in batter to form pancake
  5. Cook until bubbles form, then flip and cook until golden.

Enjoy your homemade pancakes!
